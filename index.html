About Sharma Ji Ki Chai

Located on Trilokinath Marg in Lalbagh, near Novelty Cinema in Hazratganj, Sharma Ji Ki Chai is one of Lucknow's most loved tea stalls. Students, office-goers, shoppers and early-morning walkers all stop here for a kulhad of strong chai and a plate of hot samosas before the day begins.

Simple, fresh and full of flavour — that is the promise with every cup. Whether it is a quick morning chai or an evening catch-up with friends, this is where Lucknow comes together.
